# Legal Memo Reviewer

A public, lawyer-led workflow for reviewing a **draft legal memo before relying on its recommendation**. It produces a short review, an auditable list of claim-to-source checks, and a concrete decision or next step. It does not turn the draft into a brief.

## Start here

- [Skill](SKILL.md): instructions for a reviewer or an approved AI assistant.
- [Lawyer’s guide](docs/LAWYER-GUIDE.md): setup, a first review, and acceptance checks.
- [White paper](docs/WHITE-PAPER.md): why the workflow separates source support, legal fit, and decision risk.
- [Fictional source packet](examples/01-fictional-packet.md), [draft memo](examples/02-draft-memo.md), and [worked review](examples/03-worked-review.md): an end-to-end exercise with competing timelines.
- [Review worksheet](docs/REVIEW-WORKSHEET.md): a reusable output scaffold.

You can use the skill as a human checklist or give it to a writing assistant. No software, subscription, or particular research service is required to run the fictional exercise. For an actual matter, use only material you are authorized to review in an environment approved for it. Work through the fictional example first. Do not send real matter materials to an unapproved service.

This is educational material for lawyers, not legal advice or a substitute for independent judgment or current legal research. It creates no attorney-client relationship. A review does not authorize changing originals, sending anything, or filing.
