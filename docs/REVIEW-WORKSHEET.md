# Legal memo review worksheet

Use this scaffold to create a separate review of the memo. Delete the placeholders; do not fill them with assumptions disguised as facts.

## Decision-driving findings

For each issue, record:

- **Draft proposition:** the exact assertion or section under review.
- **Record locator:** document ID/version and page, paragraph, section, or other stable location.
- **Check:** supported, partial, contradicted, disputed, or not verified; identify inference or assumption.
- **Authority locator and verification:** primary text and pinpoint, jurisdiction, posture, and material later/adverse treatment; state any gap.
- **Impact:** decision-changing, material, or cleanup; explain consequence and confidence separately.
- **Targeted correction:** one change that would keep the draft from overstating the record or law.
